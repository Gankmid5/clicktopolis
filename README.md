# 🏛️ Clicktopolis — Build History One Click at a Time

**Clicktopolis** is a web-based incremental city-building clicker game where you found a settlement in the **Stone Age** and guide it across **9 mysterious historical & futuristic Epochs** all the way to the **Cyberpunk Age**.

## ✨ Features
- **🏕️ Name Your Settlement:** Christen your tribe at the start of the game (or roll a random historical/mythic name) and watch its title evolve from *Tribe of ...* to *City-State*, *Republic*, *Metropolis*, and *Megacity*—with dynamic references in the live Chronicle news ticker.
- **🌅 Fog-of-History Progression:** Future Epochs, buildings, technologies, World Wonders, and achievements remain shrouded in mystery until your civilization approaches them—making each new age a genuine surprise with a full-screen Era Reveal celebration.
- **🎨 Full-Color Illustrated City Diorama:** An animated HTML5 Canvas landscape with layered hills, a shimmering river, drifting clouds, twinkling stars, and detailed full-color buildings—from ochre hide tents, mossy Stonehenge trilithons, and lapis-lazuli ziggurats to Roman aqueducts, medieval castles, copper-domed observatories, red-brick steam ironworks, orbital launchpads, and neon-drenched cyberpunk arcologies.
- **🏛️ 9 Monumental World Wonders & 3 State Doctrines:** Erect era-defining Wonders and switch between *Balanced Pax*, *Forge Mobilization*, and *Golden Prosperity* doctrines.
- **🔥 Momentum Combo & 💥 3x Critical Strikes:** Active clicking builds Momentum and triggers Critical Strikes with shockwave ripples and procedural Web Audio synthesizer sound effects that evolve with every Epoch.
- **🏆 100 Achievements & ⏳ Time Rift Prestige:** Unlock 100 achievements for permanent production bonuses, catch Golden Temporal Anomalies, and ascend through Time Rifts for permanent Time Shards.

## 🚀 How to Play Locally
Open `index.html` directly in any modern browser, or serve the folder locally:
```bash
python -m http.server 8080
```
Then visit `http://localhost:8080`.
