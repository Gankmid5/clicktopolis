# 🏛️ Clicktopolis — Build History One Click at a Time

[![Deploy with Vercel](https://vercel.com/button)](https://vercel.com/new/clone?repository-url=https%3A%2F%2Fgithub.com%2FGankmid5%2Fclicktopolis)

**Clicktopolis** is a zero-dependency web incremental city-building clicker game where you found a settlement in the **Stone Age** and guide it across **9 historical & futuristic Epochs** all the way to the **Cosmic Age**.

## ✨ Features
- **🏕️ Name Your Settlement:** Christen your settlement at the start of the game (or roll a random historical/mythic name) and watch its title evolve from *Tribe of ...* to *City-State*, *Republic*, *Metropolis*, and *Cosmic Arcology*—with dynamic references in the live Chronicle news ticker.
- **🎨 Scrollable City Panorama & 36 Animated District Strips:** Watch your city expand horizontally left-to-right with a slow Day/Dusk/Night/Dawn cycle, shooting stars, river boats, trains, airships, and 36 dedicated Cookie Clicker–inspired visual district strips with animated workers, smoke, waterwheels, cranes, and transit pods.
- **📜 155 Civic Upgrades Across 7 Categories:** Unlock 5 Tiers of District Upgrades, 18 Inter-District Synergies, 9 Citizen Census Ordinances, 7 Urban Sprawl Milestones, 14 Civic Festivals & Treasury Bonds, Builder Tooling, and Era Masterplans—each with category badges, live bonus readouts, and historical flavor quotes.
- **🏛️ 9 Monumental World Wonders & 3 State Doctrines:** Erect era-defining Wonders rendered directly onto your city skyline and switch between *Balanced Pax*, *Forge Mobilization*, and *Golden Prosperity* doctrines.
- **⚡ Uncapped Clicking & 💥 3x Critical Strikes:** Click as fast as you can with zero cooldown throttling, triggering Critical Strikes, shockwave ripples, and procedural Web Audio synthesizer sound effects that evolve with every Epoch.
- **🏆 100 Achievements & ⏳ Time Rift Prestige:** Unlock 100 achievements for permanent production bonuses, catch Golden Temporal Anomalies, and ascend through Time Rifts for permanent Time Shards.

## ▲ Deploy to Vercel
This repository includes a pre-configured [`vercel.json`](./vercel.json) for instant static deployment:
1. Go to [vercel.com/new](https://vercel.com/new) and import `Gankmid5/clicktopolis`.
2. Leave **Framework Preset** as `Other` and **Root Directory** as `./`.
3. Click **Deploy** — your game will be live globally in seconds!

## 🚀 How to Play Locally
Open `index.html` directly in any modern browser, or serve the folder locally:
```bash
python -m http.server 8080
```
Then visit `http://localhost:8080`.
